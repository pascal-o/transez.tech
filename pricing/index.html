Common questions

What does the free IT assessment cover?

We review how your IT is set up today, including devices, network, backups, security and your support process, and tell you what we would recommend. There is no obligation to continue.

Which plan is right for my business?

Most small teams start with Essentials or Managed. After the assessment we recommend a plan based on your team size, the systems you rely on and how much risk you want to take off your plate.

Why are prices not listed?

Plans are priced per user, and what you need depends on your environment. The free assessment lets us give you an accurate quote instead of a generic figure.

Do you handle cabling and network installs?

Yes. Low-voltage cabling, telecom and network field work are quoted per job. See our field services.

Where do you work?

We are based in Calgary, Alberta, and support businesses across Alberta and Ontario.
